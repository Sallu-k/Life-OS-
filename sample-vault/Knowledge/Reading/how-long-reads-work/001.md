A long read is a whole book cut into short passages of about a hundred and fifty words. Each time you reach for your phone for leisure, the Gate shows the next passage before anything else. It takes a minute. Over a month, those minutes add up to a book you would never have found the time to sit down with.

---

You do not have to finish a passage in one go, and you do not have to like every book. On the Knowledge page, "Make current" picks which book the Gate shows, and "Something else" on the Gate swaps a passage for a short card. The point is not to force reading, but to make it the easiest thing within reach.

---

When a line is worth keeping, save it from the Knowledge page or from Today. It goes into that book's Quotes in your vault, and some days later it comes back as a card, so the ideas you liked are the ones you remember.
