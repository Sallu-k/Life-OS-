# The Life OS mark: "A life, built"

A hexagon frame holding three faces that read as one solid block, with a small teal core where they meet.

- **The frame: six sides.** The life areas on the spider graph, and the hexagon used across the site.
- **The block: built, not found.** Flat, it is a hexagon; in depth, it is something built one day at a time.
- **Three faces.** Three shades of brass, the colour Life OS uses for what is kept.
- **The core: the spark.** A teal glow where the faces meet: you, and the energy that powers the rest.

Colours come from the home theme: charcoal `--bg` #121316, brass `--brass` #e4b565 (faces #f3d596 / #c8913a /
#7a5a2c), teal `--glow` #7fe7dc. Icon files cannot read CSS variables, so `scripts/make-icons.mjs` repeats them.

## Files (all generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs`)
- Web/PC: `public/icons/lifeos.svg`, `maskable.svg`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `maskable-512.png`,
  `lifeos.ico` (Windows shortcut), `src/app/icon.svg` (favicon), `src/app/manifest.ts` (installable app).
- Android: adaptive launcher icon (`res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml` + charcoal background + monochrome
  layer for themed icons), legacy `mipmap-*/ic_launcher*.png`, splash `ic_splash.xml`, notification icon
  `ic_stat_lifeos.xml` (white, tinted brass).

PNG sizes and the .ico are rendered from the SVGs with a headless browser (no image packages); rerun after
changing the mark.
